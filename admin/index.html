Driver-added stops

Drag the pin onto the building with the showers, then save. Open in Google Maps (satellite)

Problems with stops

Drivers at a stop said it is another chain, or that there is no truck stop. Approve fixes the stop (new chain, or off the map); Reject leaves it as it was. Two drivers saying "not here" already took it off the map until you decide.

Chains not in the app

Truck stops of other chains drivers told us about (typed in by them). Nothing was added to the map: the app has no credits for these chains.

Add a truck stop

Paste the coordinates from Google Maps (right-click the building with the showers, click the numbers to copy them), or a Google Maps link. It goes on the map at once.

Find a truck stop

Pick a state and / or a chain to see all their stops in road order (interstate, then exit); the text narrows the list. With neither picked, search by ID, town, interstate or exit - or an address, coordinates or a Google Maps link (then the stops within 30 miles, closest first). "Has showers" off: drivers see "no showers here". "Remove from map": not a truck stop at all; it can be put back.
