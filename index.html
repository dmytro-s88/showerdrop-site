How it works

  1. Pull in and request a showerThe app finds the truck stop you're at and asks drivers there with credits for that chain.
  2. A donor acceptsThey reserve a shower with their own loyalty account and send you the room and code, or a photo of it.
  3. Shower, then confirmTell the app the door opened. The donor gets thanks, and next time it might be you who shares.

Have credits you won't use?

When you park, tap I'm ready to share. For the next 30 minutes the app tells you when a driver at your stop needs a shower, and you decide whether to help.

Every shower you give shows on your counter. When you run out of credits, someone does the same for you.

Installing the beta

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps (once).
  3. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details → Install anyway. The beta isn't in the Play Store yet.
  4. New versions install over the old one; your account stays.
