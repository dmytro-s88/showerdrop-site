Be clean for free

Drivers with spare shower credits share a code with drivers who don't have them, right at the truck stop.

Your credits, any truck stop. Free, no money changes hands.

  1. Pull in and request a showerThe app finds the truck stop you're at and asks drivers there with credits for that chain.
  2. A donor acceptsThey reserve a shower with their own loyalty account and send you the room and code, or a photo of it.
  3. Shower, then confirmTell the app the door opened. The donor gets thanks, and next time it might be you who shares.

ShowerDrop is in a closed beta for Android. Questions or want to join: support@showerdrop.app
