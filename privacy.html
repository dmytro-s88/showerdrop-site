Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

ShowerDrop ("we") runs a mobile app that lets truck drivers share spare shower credits at truck stops. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how you can delete it. Questions: support@showerdrop.app.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address if you sign in with an email code or with Google (from Google we also receive your name and profile picture; other drivers never see them, only our support team sees your name, to help with your account).
  • Profile. Nickname, avatar (one of our pictures or a photo you upload), app language, the loyalty chains you have credits with, and when you accepted the Terms.
  • Location, only while the app is open. Your phone's location is used to find the truck stop you're at and to check you're within 1,000 feet of it when you request a shower, offer to share, or ask to be notified when a donor arrives. We do not store your coordinates or track your route; we store only which stop you checked in at and when, and which stop you're waiting at if you asked to be notified. We never use location in the background.
  • Exchanges. For each shower exchange: the stop, both participants, times and outcome. The shower code and any photo of it are deleted as soon as the exchange closes, unless the photo was reported (see below).
  • Notifications. A push token for your device, so we can tell you about requests and codes.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report: what went wrong in the code, the app version, technical details of the phone (model, system version, memory, battery level, language, time zone, and the country it's connecting from), the last few screens and taps before the error, and your account's random ID. No email, name, precise location, IP address or shower codes.
  • This website. We count visits to showerdrop.app with Cloudflare Web Analytics: which pages were opened, from which site or country, on what kind of device. It uses no cookies and does not identify you or follow you to other sites.
  • Reports and fair-use records. Complaints you send, and records of failed exchanges or no-shows that can pause an account for a while.

How we use it

Only to run the service: match drivers at the same stop, deliver codes and notifications, keep exchanges fair, answer support requests and keep the app working. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your data for advertising.

What other drivers see

The driver you exchange with sees your nickname and avatar, and, for the code, what the donor sends. Nobody sees your email, location or exchange history.

Reported photos

If a photo of a code is reported, we keep it to review the complaint: it is emailed to our support inbox and the copy in our storage is deleted 14 days later.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications.
  • Sentry receives crash and error reports from the app (United States).
  • Google provides Sign in with Google and the map shown in the app.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and support emails.
  • Cloudflare counts visits to this website (Cloudflare Web Analytics, no cookies).

Each of them handles data only to provide its service to us. Truck stop locations come from OpenStreetMap.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Codes and code photos are deleted when the exchange ends; a reported photo is deleted from our storage 14 days after the report is emailed to us. Sharing ends after the time you choose (30 minutes to 2 hours) or when you leave the stop; a request to be notified about a donor ends after 2 hours or once you're notified.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, photos and push tokens. Past exchanges stay only as anonymous records without your identity, so the other driver's history stays correct. If the account has an active block, strikes, a cooldown or a fair-use debt, we keep a scrambled one-way code of your email (a hash, not the address) with that record, so that deleting an account doesn't erase it: a block for 1 year, strikes and a cooldown until they end, a debt for 90 days. It applies again if the same email signs up within that time, and the code is deleted when the time is up. Accounts with a clean record leave nothing behind. You can also email support@showerdrop.app to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Children

ShowerDrop is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
